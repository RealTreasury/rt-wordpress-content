Cookie Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026
Last Updated: September 22, 2026

This page lists the cookies realtreasury.com sets, what each is for, and how long it lasts. It is meant to be checkable against what your browser actually shows you. If you find something here that we have not listed, tell us and we will either explain it or remove it.

1. The Short Version

We use two kinds of cookie. Essential ones keep the site working and are always on. Analytics and marketing ones tell us which pages get read, which companies visit, and how LinkedIn ads perform. They stay off until you switch them on.

We do not sell what these cookies collect. If you accept, LinkedIn and Apollo receive data about your visit, as described in section 3. If you reject, or never answer, none of them runs.

2. Essential Cookies

These are set because the site cannot run without them: they keep it secure, stop bots submitting our forms, and remember the cookie choice you made. We do not ask consent for these, and turning them off in your browser will break parts of the site.

NameSet byPurposeExpires
rt_consent Real Treasury Remembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics cookies 180 days
__cf_bm Cloudflare Distinguishes people from bots 30 minutes
_cfuvid Cloudflare Applies rate limits per visitor Session
cf_clearance Cloudflare Records that you passed a security challenge, so you are not asked again. Only set if you are challenged. Up to 1 year
cf_chl_* Cloudflare Turnstile Holds the state of the "are you human" check on our forms Session
wordpress_test_cookie, wp-settings-* WordPress Sign-in and admin preferences. Only set for our own logged-in staff, never for visitors. Session to 1 year

We also store one item in your browser's local storage, rt_consent_at, which is the date and time you made your cookie choice. We keep it so we can show when consent was given if you ever ask.

If you register to watch one of our recorded webinars, that page also stores rtg_<webinar>_access in local storage. It remembers that you registered, so the video plays on your next visit without asking again.

After you request our treasury technology selection guide, the thank-you page stores rt_guide_lead_sent in your browser's session storage, so a reload does not count your request twice. It is cleared when you close the tab.

3. Analytics and Marketing Cookies

These are set only after you accept them. Until then Google Analytics loads in a mode that stores nothing on your device, and the LinkedIn and Apollo tags do not load at all. If you reject, or never answer, none of them runs; if you accept and later change your mind, we delete the Google Analytics cookies below and stop loading the other two tags.

NameSet byPurposeExpires
_ga Google Analytics 4 Distinguishes one browser from another so repeat visits are not counted as new people 2 years
_ga_<property id> Google Analytics 4 Keeps the state of your current visit 2 years

LinkedIn Insight Tag. Measures how LinkedIn ads perform and lets site visitors be reached with LinkedIn ads. LinkedIn sets its own cookies, listed in LinkedIn's cookie table, and uses the data under its cookie policy.

Apollo website tracker. Matches a visit to the company it likely comes from, so we can see which organizations read our material. Apollo handles that data under its privacy policy.

Google acts as our processor for this data. It is governed by Google's privacy policy, and you can also opt out at the browser level with Google's opt-out add-on.

4. Embedded Content

Some pages embed a video from YouTube. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced (no-cookie) player, which does not set YouTube's cookies when the page loads, though the player may still keep some data in your browser's local storage. Once you press play, YouTube may set its own cookies under Google's policy. Pages that link out to LinkedIn or a vendor's website hand you over to that site's own cookie practices, which this policy does not cover.

5. Changing Your Mind

Use the cookie preferences control on this page or on our Privacy Policy. Your choice is remembered for 180 days, after which we will ask again.

You can also manage cookies in your browser directly — every major browser lets you block or delete them, usually under Settings → Privacy. Blocking essential cookies will stop our forms working.
