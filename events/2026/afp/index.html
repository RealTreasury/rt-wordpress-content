What You Get

A small, guided group – limited to 10 treasury professionals per tour – visits three vendor stops in 75 minutes.

During the stops at each booth, every vendor follows the same script, covers the same topic, in the same amount of time.

Between stops, we talk through what you just saw: what mattered, what didn’t, and what was different. After the third demo, you have a real basis for comparison – three vendors evaluated against identical criteria, with independent guides who have been on both sides of this process.

We do not accept payment from any TMS vendor for any of these tours. Every vendor on each tour receives the same invitation.

The Two Tours

TMS Replacement

For teams already running a treasury system and weighing a change. The route visits vendors that could replace what you have, and every stop runs the same scenario — so what differs between them is the product rather than the pitch.

For teams running treasury on spreadsheets and bank portals, where the honest answer to “where is our cash right now” takes half a morning. The route visits vendors that solve that first, and seeing the same scenario three times is the quickest way to tell a working capability from a promise.

Good to Know

Is it really free?

Yes. Attendees don’t pay, vendors don’t pay.

Which vendors are on my route?

Routes are grouped by focus, not published vendor by vendor. Every TMS vendor on our market map with an AFP booth is invited, and each appears on one route. Your route is built around your situation — replacing an existing system or improving treasury visibility — so you meet with three candidates selected around your needs.

Will vendors know I’m coming?

Vendors get the attendee list shortly before the tour. Prefer to evaluate quietly? Request a Custom Tour.

What if none of the times work for me?

Select None of These Work for Me when you register and leave your details. We’ll reach out to see what may work.

Am I signing up to be sold something?

No. Nothing is bought or decided on a tour, and the debrief is vendor-free by design.
