Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Attendees don’t pay, vendors don’t pay.

Which vendors are on the route?

Routes are grouped by focus, not published vendor by vendor. Every TMS vendor on our market map with an AFP booth is invited, and each appears on one route.

Will vendors know I’m coming?

Vendors get the attendee list shortly before the tour. Prefer to evaluate quietly? Request a private tour.

These times don’t work for me.

Request a private tour — we schedule those individually across both days.

Am I signing up to be sold something?

No. Nothing is bought or decided on a tour, and the debrief is vendor-free by design.
