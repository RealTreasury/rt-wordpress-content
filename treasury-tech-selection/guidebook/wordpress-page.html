This is not just another guide!

When you first saw or heard about this TMS Selection Guide you probably thought, “another one?”

But wait! This one is different. This guide contains EVERY TMS vendor selling into the North American treasury marketplace.

What’s in the guide

This guide is designed to help you understand the TMS marketplace better and includes:

  • An article about our proprietary ERR NOT™ selection methodology with tips you can use to improve your selection process;
  • Information about the three segments and the products in each;
  • A matrix of each segment that highlights each product’s functionality; and
  • Several articles which are designed to give you information about changing technology and to help you make better selection decisions.

Why this one is different

If you look up “how to buy a treasury management system,” you’ll find plenty of guides from consultants and industry analysts where vendors pay for inclusion. Moreover, many vendors have written selection guides and, surprisingly enough, each one is biased towards its own system.

This Selection Guide is an unbiased view of the current TMS marketplace, not just a narrow slice of the market. Like the TMS marketplace chart, this guide includes every vendor that meets our segment definitions. EVERY ONE and without any kind of financial consideration whatsoever.

At Real Treasury, vendors do not pay for inclusion in our market charts, our Treasury Tech Portal, or this guide. Every vendor on our marketplace chart is included in the segment listings and in the matrix. It’s entirely free.

We believe that knowledge of the segments, and the products within each segment, gives you a better view of your available options so that you can find the best-fit product to meet your unique requirements.

Our principles

A little more than two years ago we started Real Treasury with certain principles in mind, our “Requirements,” the guideposts or North Star we continue to depend on to drive our decisions:

  • We work exclusively for our clients. We are your advocates, bringing our unique skillsets and prior experiences to bear on your behalf.
  • We are objective and vendor-agnostic.
  • We don’t let partnerships influence selection advice. We have no partnerships with TMS vendors that generate referral payments, kickbacks, commissions, implementation revenue, or any kind of payment that could influence our advice to you.

These principles differentiate us from the many other treasury consultants available to you.
