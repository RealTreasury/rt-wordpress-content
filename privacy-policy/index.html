Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 21, 2025
Last Updated: September 22, 2026

Real Treasury, LLC is an independent advisory firm for corporate treasury teams. This policy covers realtreasury.com. It describes what the website collects, who processes it on our behalf, and what you can ask us to do about it.

We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not receive fees from technology vendors.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form: first name, last name, email address, company, and the message you write.
  • Newsletter signup: your email address, the page you signed up from, and the date and time you opted in.
  • Gated content (the selection guide, Treasury Tech Portal access, webinar and workshop registrations): your name, work email, company, job title, and phone number where the form asks for it, along with your consent to be contacted.
  • Email and meetings: anything you send us directly, and the scheduling details if you book a call.

We do not operate user accounts on this website. There is no username or password to create, and we do not take payment through the site.

Information collected automatically

  • Server and security data: your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you request. Our CDN and firewall need these to serve the site and block abuse.
  • Gated-form submissions: we store a one-way hash of the submitting IP address and browser user agent, not the raw values, to detect automated abuse of our forms.
  • How you reached a gated page: when you submit a gated form, we record the campaign tags on the link you followed, the page you landed on, and the referring site's domain with your submission, whether or not you accept analytics cookies. If you accept them, your browser also keeps this in session storage until you close the tab, so a form on a later page records where your visit started; if you do not, it is kept for the current page only.
  • Analytics and marketing: pages viewed, time on page, referring source, the company your visit likely comes from, and whether you arrived from a LinkedIn ad — only if you accept analytics and marketing cookies in the cookie banner. They stay off until you do. Before you choose, and if you decline, Google Analytics still receives a cookieless signal for each page view (the page, your IP address, and browser type) with no cookie set.

4. Cookies

Analytics and marketing cookies are switched off until you accept them. Essential cookies — the ones that keep the site secure, filter form spam, and remember your cookie choice — are always on, because the site does not work without them.

Our Cookie Policy lists every cookie we set and who sets it. You can change your mind at any time using the cookie preferences control.

5. How Long We Keep It

  • Newsletter subscription: until you unsubscribe. After that we keep your address on a suppression list so we do not email you again.
  • Gated-content and enquiry records: three years from our last contact with you, then deleted.
  • Email correspondence: retained as business records, normally seven years.
  • Analytics data: Google Analytics retains user-level data for 14 months.
  • Hashed form metadata: 12 months.
  • Server and security logs: retained by our hosting and CDN providers under their own schedules, typically under 30 days.

6. Your Rights

Wherever you are, you can ask us to give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter using the link in any email, without asking us.

UK and EU (GDPR)

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection to processing based on legitimate interests. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time; that does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it. You may also complain to your national data protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA)

In the last 12 months we have collected the following categories of personal information: identifiers (name, email address, IP address), professional information (employer, job title), commercial information (what you requested from us), and internet activity (pages viewed; with analytics enabled, and as a cookieless page-view signal to Google Analytics when it is not). We collect it from you directly and from your use of the site, for the business purposes in section 2, and we disclose it to the service providers in section 3.

We have not sold personal information in the last 12 months, and we do not do so. In the last 12 months we have shared internet activity (pages viewed) with LinkedIn through the LinkedIn Insight Tag described in section 3, which can count as sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can opt out of that sharing by rejecting analytics and marketing cookies, or switching them off later. We do not collect sensitive personal information as the CPRA defines it, so there is nothing to limit the use of. You have the right to know, delete, correct, opt out of sharing, and to be free from retaliation for exercising those rights.

Making a request

Email privacy@realtreasury.com. We will acknowledge within 10 business days and respond within 30 days, or tell you why we need longer. We may ask you to confirm the email address the request relates to, so that we do not hand your data to someone else.

7. Security

The site runs over TLS. Form submissions go through a bot check before they reach us. Access to the systems holding enquiry records is limited to the two principals of the firm and protected by multi-factor authentication. Our hosting, CDN and email providers maintain their own security programs and certifications.

We are a two-person firm and we would rather be accurate than impressive: we do not run a formal certified information security program of our own. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Where Your Data Goes

We are based in the United States and our service providers process data in the United States. If you are in the UK or EEA, your information is transferred outside your country. Those transfers rely on the transfer mechanisms our providers have in place — principally the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum, incorporated into each provider's data processing terms, and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified.

9. Children

This site is for treasury and finance professionals. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

10. Other Sites

We link to vendor websites, LinkedIn profiles, and other third-party pages. This policy does not cover them. Our Treasury Tech Portal shows information about technology vendors; visiting the portal does not share your details with any vendor unless you ask us to introduce you.

11. Changes

When we change this policy we update the "Last Updated" date above. If a change materially affects how we handle information you have already given us, we will tell subscribers by email and, where the law requires consent, ask for it.
